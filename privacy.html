Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Angela Lawlor Ltd, trading as UK Manufacturing Festivals, collects, uses, stores and protects personal information when you visit our website, contact us, participate in one of our events, or otherwise interact with us.

1. Who We Are

UK Manufacturing Festivals is the trading name of Angela Lawlor Ltd, which organises one-day manufacturing festivals throughout the United Kingdom. Our events bring together manufacturers and local schools to inspire young people by showcasing the wide range of careers available within manufacturing and engineering through engaging, hands-on activities.

2. Information We Collect

Business Representatives

We may collect:

  • Company name
  • Company address
  • Company telephone number
  • Company email address
  • Company logo
  • Names and job titles of employees
  • Business contact telephone numbers
  • Business email addresses

Schools

We may collect:

  • School name
  • School address
  • School website
  • Names of teachers, careers leaders and other school contacts
  • School telephone numbers
  • School email addresses
  • A personal mobile telephone number for the lead teacher attending the event. This is collected solely so that we can contact them urgently on the day of the event should an emergency or unforeseen issue arise. It is not used for marketing purposes.

Photography, Video and Promotional Material

During our events we may take photographs and record video footage that may include exhibitors, teachers, volunteers and attendees. These may be used for:

  • Our website
  • Social media platforms
  • Printed promotional materials
  • Future event marketing
  • Press releases and publicity

We may also use exhibitor company logos to promote our events before, during and after each festival.

3. How We Collect Information

We collect personal information when you:

  • Contact us through our website enquiry form
  • Contact us by email or telephone
  • Register a school or business to participate in an event
  • Complete one of our online registration forms
  • Communicate with us during the planning and delivery of an event
  • Attend one of our events

Our website enquiry form is provided by Formspree, and our school registration forms are provided by Cognito Forms.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

  • Organise and manage our manufacturing festivals
  • Communicate with exhibitors, sponsors and participating schools
  • Respond to enquiries
  • Coordinate event logistics
  • Maintain records of organisations we work with
  • Promote current and future UK Manufacturing Festivals
  • Publish photographs, videos and exhibitor logos for promotional and publicity purposes
  • Send occasional email updates about future events or opportunities that we believe may be relevant to our existing contacts

Recipients may opt out of receiving these updates at any time by contacting us at admin@ukmanufacturing
festivals.co.uk.

5. Lawful Basis for Processing

We process personal information where:

  • It is necessary for our legitimate interests in organising and promoting our events.
  • It is necessary to fulfil arrangements with participating organisations.
  • We have obtained consent where consent is the appropriate legal basis.

6. Photography and Filming

Photography and video are an important part of showcasing the impact and success of our events.

Participating schools are responsible for obtaining any parental or guardian permissions required under their own safeguarding, photography and data protection policies.

Schools must notify UK Manufacturing Festivals before the event of any pupils who must not be photographed or filmed and provide a practical method of identifying those pupils to our event team.

Where no such information is provided before the event, UK Manufacturing Festivals will reasonably assume that the school has obtained the necessary permissions for pupils attending the event to appear in photographs and video recordings used for promotional, marketing and publicity purposes.

We will make every reasonable effort to respect any restrictions that are communicated to us.

UK Manufacturing Festivals is committed to safeguarding children and young people. Our approach to photography and filming forms part of our wider safeguarding arrangements.

7. Company Logos

We may display the logos of organisations that have exhibited at, sponsored or otherwise supported UK Manufacturing Festivals on our website, social media channels and promotional materials.

This may include logos from both current and previous events to recognise their involvement and to promote UK Manufacturing Festivals and future festivals.

If you would prefer your organisation's logo not to be used in this way, please contact us and we will consider your request.

8. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information.

We may share information where necessary with:

  • Participating schools and exhibitors where appropriate for organising events
  • Professional advisers
  • Regulatory or public authorities where required by law
  • Trusted service providers who assist us in delivering our services

These providers currently include:

  • Formspree (website enquiries)
  • Cognito Forms (school registrations)
  • Google Maps (displaying event locations)
  • Font Awesome (website icons)

9. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website does not use cookies for advertising, analytics or user accounts.

However, certain third-party services used on our website, such as Google Maps, may collect limited technical information (including your IP address) or use their own technologies when their content is loaded. These services operate under their own privacy policies.

10. Data Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

Although we take reasonable precautions, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to:

  • Organise and deliver our events
  • Maintain ongoing relationships with participating organisations
  • Meet legal, accounting or regulatory obligations
  • Promote future events where appropriate

Photographs, videos and promotional materials may be retained as part of our historical archive and for ongoing promotional purposes unless there is a legal obligation to remove them.

12. Your Rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information where applicable
  • Request restriction of processing
  • Object to certain processing activities
  • Request transfer of your information where applicable
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies upon consent

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at admin@ukmanufacturing
festivals.co.uk.

13. Complaints

If you have any concerns about how we handle your personal information, please contact us first so that we can try to resolve the matter.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
