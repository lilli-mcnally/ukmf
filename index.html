The UK manufacturing sector stands at a critical crossroads

With a significant proportion of the current workforce approaching retirement, the industry faces a growing skills gap that threatens future innovation and productivity.

For decades, manufacturing and engineering have been the backbone of the economy—but without a new generation ready to step in, that legacy is at risk.

This is where YOU come in

Engaging young people has never been more important. Modern manufacturing is high-tech, creative, and full of opportunity, yet outdated perceptions often prevent students from seeing it as a viable and exciting career path.

By showcasing real-world innovation, and providing hands-on experiences, we can inspire the next generation to explore careers in engineering and manufacturing.

UK Manufacturing Festivals exist to bridge this gap

We currently deliver two highly successful Manufacturing Festivals each year—one in Telford and one in Sandwell—bringing together local industry, educators, and young people to celebrate and promote careers in manufacturing.

Each festival is a focused, high-impact one-day event designed to inspire the next generation and showcase the opportunities within the sector and we need local manufacturers to get involved.

Latest News

7th May 2026

Exciting news! 🤝

We've joined forces with Telford Together, a collaboration of Telford & Wrekin Council, Harper Adams University and Telford College.

Their vision to strengthen and co-ordinate engagement between local schools and businesses perfectly aligns with what we're hoping to achieve with our upcoming Shropshire & Telford Manufacturing Festival, and we're thrilled to be working in partnership with them.

"Manufacturing is a vital part of Telford and Wrekin's identity and future, and this event will give young people the chance to see first-hand the exciting careers and opportunities it can offer.

By bringing schools and businesses together through Telford Together, and working with partners such as UK Manufacturing Festivals, we're helping to inspire ambition, build confidence and create clearer pathways from education into local jobs."

Councillor Shirley Reynolds

Telford & Wrekin Council Cabinet Member for Children & Young People, Learning, Employment & Skills

20th April 2026

It's Official! 🎉

On the 24th November, we'll be hosting the Shropshire and Telford Manufacturing Festival, bringing together over 700 students from local schools with some of the region's most outstanding and diverse manufacturers.

This event is all about showcasing the exciting and varied career opportunities within the manufacturing industry, helping young people see what's possible and connect directly with employers.

Following the huge success of the West Midlands Manufacturing Festival, which has already helped place ambitious young people into manufacturing apprenticeships, we're proud to be bringing that momentum to Shropshire.

Join us, and together, we'll inspire the next generation of talent and strengthen the future of manufacturing across the region.

5th March 2026

What an amazing day! 🙌

Last Thursday we delivered the West Midlands Manufacturing Festival — and what a day it was.

Bringing together manufacturers, educators, partners and young people from across the region, the event was all about showcasing the very best of our sector — the innovation, the opportunity and, most importantly, the people behind it.

Seeing the conversations taking place with the students and the genuine excitement about careers in manufacturing is exactly why we created this festival. Our industry has an incredible story to tell, and it was fantastic to see so many organisations step up to help tell it.

A huge thank you to every exhibitor, sponsor, supporter, partner and volunteer who made it happen. Events like this are never a solo effort — they're powered by collaboration.
